# Financial and Activity Reports

HAI Accounting offers a comprehensive reporting suite divided into 10 categories, enabling business owners, accountants, and administrators to gain insights into financial statements, sales, receivables, payables, purchases, inventory status, valuation, and tax compliance.

## General Reporting Features
1. **Basis of Accounting**: Reports can be toggled between:
   - **Accrual Basis**: Records revenue and expenses when they are earned or incurred, regardless of cash flow.
   - **Cash Basis**: Records revenue and expenses only when cash changes hands (shows accrual equivalents if cash basis calculations are pending).
2. **Date Filters & Presets**: Easily filter report data using built-in presets:
   - Today, This Week, This Month, This Quarter, This Year, This Financial Year, Last Month, Last Quarter, Last Year, or Custom date ranges.
3. **Advanced Filters**: Filter by status, specific vendors, or specific customers depending on the report type.
4. **Export Formats**: Export clean report datasets to multiple formats:
   - **CSV**: Standard comma-separated format.
   - **XLS / XLSX**: Microsoft Excel sheet format.
   - **PDF**: Portable Document Format, supporting custom page orientations (Portrait or Landscape).
   - **Zoho Sheet**: Direct link to open the data in Zoho Sheet, with data automatically copied to the system clipboard in TSV format for instant pasting.

## Category 1: Financial Statements
- **Trial Balance**: Lists closing debit and credit balances for all accounts as of a selected date, categorized by root type (Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, Expense) to verify that total debits equal total credits.
- **Profit & Loss**: Displays revenue generated and expenses incurred over a specific period, showing the net profit or loss.
- **Balance Sheet**: A snapshot of the organization's assets, liabilities, and equity as of a specific date.
- **Control Reconciliation**: Compares General Ledger control account balances (Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable) against the sub-ledger balances (detailed customer/vendor balances) to identify and reconcile differences.

## Category 2: Activity Reports
- **Account Transactions**: A granular ledger ledger report showing all transaction details, postings, document types, reference numbers, debits, credits, and running balances for individual accounts.

## Category 3: Sales Reports
- **Sales by Customer**: Aggregates total invoices and sales amounts (before and after tax) for each customer.
- **Sales by Item**: Summarizes total quantities sold, total revenue, and invoice count for each catalog item.
- **Sales by Item Details**: A detailed log of individual item sales including dates, invoice numbers, customers, quantities, rates, and invoice status.

## Category 4: Receivables Reports
- **Customer Balance Summary**: Lists opening balances and total outstanding receivables for every customer.
- **Customer Balance Details**: A chronological transaction ledger for each customer detailing debits, credits, and cumulative outstanding balances.
- **AR Aging Summary**: Segregates customer outstanding receivables into age buckets (Current, 1-15 days, 16-30 days, 31-45 days, and Above 45 days) to monitor overdue invoices.
- **AR Aging Details**: Shows individual unpaid invoices, their age in days, due dates, original amounts, and balance due.

## Category 5: Payments Received Reports
- **Payments Received Summary**: Summarizes payments collected from customers, detailing total amount received, amount applied to invoices, and unapplied (excess) balances.

## Category 6: Payables Reports
- **Vendor Balance Summary**: Lists opening balances and total outstanding payables for each vendor.
- **AP Aging Summary**: Segregates vendor outstanding payables into age buckets (Current, 1-15 days, 16-30 days, 31-45 days, and Above 45 days) to help plan cash outflows.

## Category 7: Purchases & Expenses
- **Purchases by Vendor**: Summarizes total purchases and bill counts (before and after tax) for each vendor.
- **Purchases by Item Details**: Tracks granular item purchases including bill dates, bill numbers, vendors, quantities, and rates.
- **Expense Details**: A comprehensive log of expenses, detailing expense accounts, vendors, customers (if billable), tax amounts, totals, payment modes, and payment status.

## Category 8: Inventory Reports
- **Inventory Summary**: Tracks stock status including SKU, reorder levels, quantity ordered, quantities in/out, stock on hand, committed stock (linked to open sales orders), and available stock.
- **Committed Stock Details**: Details items committed to open Sales Orders, specifying order dates, expected shipment, customer, quantity, and amount.
- **Inventory Aging Summary**: Segregates inventory into age buckets based on acquisition dates.
- **Stock Summary**: Grouped stock status summary showing total items, quantities, committed/available stock, and asset value.
- **Inventory Adjustment Summary**: Aggregates inventory adjustments grouped by reason.
- **Inventory Adjustment Details**: Individual log of adjustments showing date, warehouse, direction (increase/decrease), delta quantity/value, and resulting stock on hand.
- **Packing History**: Logs packing actions showing delivery challan numbers, sales orders, customer, packed quantities, amounts, and statuses.
- **Shipment Details**: Tracks shipping status of items from delivery challans.
- **Inventory Turnover By Quantity**: Calculates inventory turnover ratio based on average quantity and sales issue rates.

## Category 9: Inventory Valuation
- **Inventory Valuation Summary**: Summarizes the valuation of items, showing the valuation method (e.g., Average Cost), average cost, total inventory value, and value share percentage.
- **FIFO Cost Lot Tracking**: Tracks cost lots for FIFO inventory, detailing lot age, quantities, unit costs, and lot values.
- **ABC Classification**: Classifies inventory into A, B, and C categories based on sales share and cumulative percentages to help prioritize inventory control.
- **Inventory Turnover By Amount**: Calculates turnover ratios, COGS, gross margins, and margin percentages relative to average inventory values.

## Category 10: GST Reports
- **HSN-wise Summary (GSTR-1/GSTR-3B)**: Summarizes sales transactions grouped by HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) or SAC (Services Accounting Code) with quantity, taxable value, and tax breakdown (IGST, CGST, SGST/UTGST, Cess).
